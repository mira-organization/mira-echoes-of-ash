Iron Death Scythe

Lore Description

Legends say this scythe was used for most field work. However, it was used in a gruesome ritual on old believers of the Church of Light. Their faces were cut off while they were still alive. It is said that the screams of the ritual victims can still be heard today. Scythe wielders hear voices telling them to gather more. But no one really knows what this means...
